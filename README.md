## Why a Tube Can Be the Right Packaging Shape

A tube changes the way long items are protected. Instead of folding a document or forcing a poster into a flat package, the material can remain rolled while surrounded by a rigid protective wall.

Mailing tubes can be useful for:

* Posters and promotional graphics
* Architectural drawings and blueprints
* Artwork and prints
* Documents and certificates
* Maps and charts
* Banners and rolled materials
* Long, narrow products

The right diameter and length allow the contents to sit securely without unnecessary movement.

## Cardboard That Keeps Materials Protected

Cardboard mailing tubes are a popular choice for applications where lightweight protection and easy handling are important. Spiral-wound paperboard and corrugated constructions are available in different configurations, while end caps can help secure the contents during transportation.

The tube can also make storage easier because several units can be stacked or organized without taking up the same footprint as oversized flat packaging.

### Small Details That Matter

When selecting a tube, pay attention to:

* Inside diameter
* Overall length
* Wall construction
* End closure style
* Product weight
* Shipping conditions
* Required quantity

These details can affect how easily the product fits and how well it remains protected throughout handling.
